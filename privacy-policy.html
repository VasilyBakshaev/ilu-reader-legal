Privacy Policy

Operator: Ilu Reader project. Contact: vasily.bakshaev@gmail.com.
Publication: https://vasilybakshaev.github.io/ilu-reader-legal/privacy-policy.html. Effective date: November 23, 2026.

1. In brief

Ilu Reader is a book reader with tap-to-translate. Books, settings, reading progress and word cards are stored on the device. Only the requests needed for AI translation, subscription checks and loading the Cloud catalog are sent online.

2. What data is processed

On the device: books, settings, reading progress, book language, word cards, translation cache and a random device identifier (16 random bytes; no name, email or phone number).

On our server (for AI translation): device identifier, word, context sentence and translation languages. The identifier is used only for limits and subscriptions and is not sent to the AI service. The word and sentence are not stored after the response — only request counters remain.

Subscription: subscription status is processed through RevenueCat, payment — through the App Store or Google Play. We do not receive bank card data.

Technical data: IP address and standard HTTP headers are visible to Cloudflare (hosting), RevenueCat, the AI provider and GitHub (Cloud catalog).

We do not collect names, email, phone numbers, location, contacts, photos or advertising identifiers, do not show ads and do not track users across apps and websites.

3. Why

  • operation of the reader, on-device translation and cards;
  • AI translation of a word in context and limit accounting;
  • subscription checks;
  • protecting the server from spam and abuse;
  • responding to support requests.

6. Your rights

Local data is deleted together with the app. Server data (counters, subscription cache, RevenueCat record) is deleted on request to vasily.bakshaev@gmail.com; we reply within 30 days.

7. Children

The app is not intended for children under 12 and does not collect age data.

8. On-device mode

If translation on the device is enabled in Settings, the word and sentence are not sent to our server or the AI: translation runs on the device. Requests to the Cloud catalog and subscription checks may still be performed.

9. Changes

A new version is published at https://vasilybakshaev.github.io/ilu-reader-legal/privacy-policy.html and in the app; the effective date is shown at the beginning of the document.
